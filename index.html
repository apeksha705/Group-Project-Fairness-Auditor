WorkLens analyzes activity across GitHub, shared docs, task boards, and meeting logs, then converts it into a transparent, per-member contribution report. Built for instructors, teaching assistants, and student teams who need evidence, not guesswork, when it's time to grade group work.

Any project source

GitHub, Google Docs, and task boards connect directly — no manual activity logging.

5 contribution signals

Commits, doc edits, task completions, reviews, and meeting participation, weighted together.

Visual breakdowns

Member-by-member charts make uneven effort visible at a glance.

Explainable reports

Every score comes with plain-language evidence behind it — nothing is a black box.
